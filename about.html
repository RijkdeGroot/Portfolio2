About Me

Designing worlds where narrative meets dimension.

I’d like to create something that sparks joy into the heart of many and inspirers even Beter ideas within the next generation. Now that would be a story worth telling.

Hi, My name is Rijk de Groot. a CMD student at Avans Breda. As a designer I realised early on that I prefer to weave my own tales and stories, come up with my own concepts and enjoy working with 3-dimensional forms of art. I adhere to a code of only working on maximumly 3 projects at the same time, although I prefer for it to be one so each project can get the attention it deserves and needs. I aspire to one day have my own animated show and to share my story ideas with the world!

The Three Pillars

01. Conceptualising

Translating vague impulses, ambitious briefs, and thematic questions into robust design systems, visual bibles, and tangible direction.

02. Storytelling

Crafting emotional resonance, character perspectives, and spatial narrative. Giving environments purpose and an underlying soul.

03. 3D Design

Transforming ideas into high-fidelity volumetric environments, procedural assets, cinematic lighting scenes, and kinetic forms using Blender and modern 3D pipelines.
